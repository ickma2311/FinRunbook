Analyst view

The buildout is widening, but disclosure quality is uneven across the stack.

The spend cycle broadened from accelerators into memory, networking and physical infrastructure. The clearest disclosed customer exposure remains concentrated in NVIDIA, Arista and selected custom-silicon programs. Upstream foundry, HBM and cooling vendors show strong operating leverage, but their revenue overlaps and is not a market-size total.

Evidence supporting the view
  • All four buyers publicly committed to NVIDIA Blackwell adoption or deployment.
  • Arista directly quantifies Microsoft and Meta as major end customers.
  • TSMC HPC, memory margins, and power/cooling backlog all accelerated.
Conditions that would weaken the view
  • Capex is broader than AI and may not map one-for-one to supplier revenue.
  • Custom accelerators can shift value between GPU and ASIC suppliers.
  • Backlog cancellations, delivery bottlenecks or mix can weaken conversion and margins.

Value flow: four lenses, no false total

The same physical deployment appears at several layers and on different recognition dates.

1 · Buyer capex

Buildings, servers, networking and other equipment acquired by hyperscalers.

2 · Platform revenue

Accelerators, systems, custom silicon and networking sold into deployments.

3 · Upstream revenue

Foundry and HBM embedded inside the platform products above.

4 · Facility revenue

Power, UPS and cooling supporting the same datacenter load.

Non-additivity rule

Do not sum the eight suppliers' revenue or AI-related KPIs and label the result end-market size. The measures overlap across layers, mix currencies and use different fiscal periods.

Methodology and limitations

Definitions
  • Capex: cash PPE additions or purchases; Meta adds finance-lease principal and Amazon is net of proceeds/incentives.
  • Supplier financials: reported GAAP or local GAAP; Eaton uses Electrical Americas for relevance.
  • Relationship: both parties must be named by an issuer, filing or regulator.
Limitations
  • Hyperscaler capex includes non-AI infrastructure.
  • Named adoption does not disclose supplier revenue concentration.
  • Latest quarters differ; SK hynix Q2 2026 was preliminary.
  • Backlog and order definitions differ by supplier.
