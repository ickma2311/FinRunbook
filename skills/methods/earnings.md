# Earnings analysis

Confirm fiscal quarter, exact dates and release/filing vintage. Keep reported
results, prior management guidance, timestamped pre-release consensus, and new
guidance separate. Missing consensus stays unavailable; current estimates are
not historical consensus.

Compare revenue, gross profit, operating income, EPS, operating cash flow and
material sector/segment KPIs with the same quarter and appropriate sequential
period. Do not compare YTD cash flow with quarterly income. Derive a quarter
from YTD only when scope and restatement basis match; record both inputs.

Reconcile GAAP and adjusted results through disclosed items. Preserve dilution,
splits, tax effects and adjusted definitions. Distinguish cash charges from
noncash exclusions. Recurring exclusions merit analysis rather than automatic
normalization.

Quantify changes with a reconciled bridge: revenue at prior gross margin,
margin change at current revenue, then operating expense change. This is an
arithmetic decomposition, not a causal estimate of price or volume. Use disclosed
FX, acquisitions, units or price/mix for attribution, leaving a residual when
unexplained.

Guidance retains range, midpoint, issue date, assumptions and fiscal period.
Account for elapsed time and actual results when comparing guidance revisions.
Identify KPIs that would test the outlook. A beat or stock move does not by
itself establish durable operating improvement. Substantial reports need
comparable results, reconciliations, historical context and outlook implications.
