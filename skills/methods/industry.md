# Industry and competitor analysis

Define geography, value-chain boundary and inclusion rule before company
selection. Include relevant business models and contrary examples. Separate
private/listed coverage and disclose missing statements.

Build common financial panels for revenue, margins, cash generation and capital
intensity where comparable. Use separate sector KPIs for the mechanism:
subscribers, retention, usage, utilization, backlog, pricing, production or
unit costs. ARR, bookings, revenue, penetration and market share differ.

Align periods, currencies, acquisitions, restatements, segment allocations and
changing product boundaries. Do not assign a broader segment's profit to an
undisclosed product. Keep incompatible metrics in separate panels and missing
data explicit. Substantial comparisons need historical context beyond one latest
growth rate.

Explain the customer budget or activity that changes and how spending could
move among suppliers, customers, internal builds or eliminated activity. Use
measured amounts where supported. Growth differences do not prove share transfer;
directional indicators do not form a 100% waterfall. A share proxy needs a
definition and consistent denominator.

Rank exposures to the mechanism with financial reasons and observations that
could reverse the ranking. Distinguish industry growth from firm performance.
Assess cyclicality, inputs, concentration, regulation, substitution and financing
as relevant; identify offsets. Deliver comparable tables, a bridge or exposure
ranking and forward indicators. Add valuation only when price is in scope.
